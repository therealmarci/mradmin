# Tickets & reports (/report)

You saw a rule violation, found a bug or have a question for the team? **`/report`** (also `/melden`, `/ticket`, `/support`) opens the **ticket menu**:

- **New ticket:** choose a category (e.g. cheating, chat, griefing, scam, bug, question/other), then fill in a short form. For a player report you choose the player first. Your location is saved with it so the team finds the spot.
- **Open tickets:** new answers from the team glow.
- **Archive:** your closed tickets.

`/report <player>` jumps straight to choosing the reason.

## What happens next?
- The ticket shows the history like a chat - you answer in the same menu.
- If you are offline, you learn about new answers at your next login.
- You can close your ticket yourself when the matter is settled.
- Some tickets are created for you automatically by a plugin - e.g. when your chunk loader was locked because of lag. Only the team closes those.

> [!TIP]
> With the [player page](spielerseite.md) you also see and answer your tickets in the browser.
